THREAT MODEL
Assets: customer funds/claims/loan decisions, credentials, identity data, account and payment references, internal keys, models, business continuity. Threat actors may use phishing, remote-access scams, SIM swap, credential stuffing, card-not-present fraud, fake QR/collect requests, insider misuse, malware, API abuse, data exfiltration or provider compromise. A threat model identifies actor, entry point, privilege, impact and control; it is not enough to label everything “cyber fraud.”

COMMON INDIAN USER-FACING PAYMENT SCAMS
Fake support asks for UPI PIN/OTP or installs screen-sharing app; QR code is presented as a way to receive money even though scanning/approving can authorize a payment; collect request is mistaken for refund; fake loan app harvests contacts/photos; SIM replacement enables account recovery attacks; merchant impersonation changes payout details; phishing copies bank/UPI login. Receiving funds does not require disclosing PIN/CVV/OTP. Banks or genuine support should not require remote-control access or secrecy credentials to reverse a payment.

FRAUD DETECTION PIPELINE
Event intake -> identity/device/account/merchant/counterparty enrichment -> feature and velocity calculation -> deterministic rules and/or risk model -> risk score/reason codes -> step-up auth, hold, decline or manual review within policy -> feedback/confirmed outcomes -> rule/model monitoring. Signals can include new device, rate of beneficiary creation, amount relative to history, IP/session anomalies, compromised credentials, merchant behavior and graph relationships. These are probabilistic indicators; a model score is not proof. Monitor false positives, false negatives, bias, drift, latency, appeal and control override.

TECHNICAL CONTROLS
TLS and secure certificate validation; encryption at rest; key management/HSM where required; secrets vault and rotation; least privilege; segregation of duties; MFA for operations; signed/time-bounded webhooks; nonce/replay defense; input validation; rate limits; secure dependency/supply-chain management; patching; backups; tested disaster recovery; network segmentation; audit log integrity; data minimization and retention. Mask credentials and account identifiers in logs; never persist CVV/PIN/OTP. Tokenization replaces sensitive card identifier with a scoped token but does not automatically remove all PCI/RBI/privacy duties.

PRIVACY AND CONSENT IN SYSTEM DESIGN
Track data source, purpose, lawful basis/consent, permitted recipient, scope, retention period, access history and deletion/retention exception. Data minimization means collect only what is required for the disclosed service/control. Consent should not be bundled deceptively; withdrawal may stop future processing but does not automatically erase records that law requires a regulated entity to retain. India data protection implementation is subject to the applicable Act, rules, commencement notifications and sector directions; do not state a rule effective date without checking official Gazette.

INCIDENT RESPONSE WORKFLOW
Detect/triage -> protect customer and funds (freeze or step-up only under authority) -> preserve forensic evidence -> notify bank/regulated entity/operator/security contacts under applicable clock -> contain credentials/tokens and rotate keys -> assess scope and affected transactions/data -> customer/regulator/LEA notices when legally required -> restore clean services -> reconcile uncertain transactions -> document root cause, impact and corrective action. A service outage, cyber incident, payment fraud report and personal data breach can trigger different reporting regimes.

CUSTOMER LOSS/DISPUTE HANDLING
Capture date/time, channel, amount, payment rail, UTR/RRN, account entry, device/call/message evidence, bank complaint ID and actions taken. Customer should contact bank/payment provider via official channels promptly; account-specific liability rules depend on product, customer conduct and applicable RBI/regulator directions. Do not guarantee recovery or chargeback. National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in/; RBI CMS for eligible bank/payment complaints: https://cms.rbi.org.in/.

REGULATORY CONTEXT
RBI Master Directions index hosts payment system, digital payment security, PPI and other current directions: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasterDirections.aspx
RBI issued 2024 Master Directions on Cyber Resilience and Digital Payment Security Controls for non-bank Payment System Operators; see RBI payment-system notifications for exact scope and staged effective dates. This source was not checked clause by clause here. Apply control requirements by regulated entity, service, size/category and commencement date rather than importing one checklist into every startup.
