VERIFICATION METHOD
Primary regulator, scheme, or government source pages were checked for the topics below on 2026-10-09. This date records this collection's source review, not confirmation that each page contains a consolidated, current statement of every applicable requirement. A listed source supports a topic; it does not automatically validate every statement in every document. Verify circular status, amendments, effective dates, and entity scope before relying on a time-sensitive rule.

URL: https://www.rbi.org.in/
Supports: regulated banks/NBFCs/payment systems, master directions, circulars, consumer FAQs.
Specific references:

OPEN VERIFICATION GAPS
1. Clause-by-clause requirements/effective dates under RBI Digital Lending Directions 2025.
2. Current RBI UPI/card failed transaction compensation/reversal directions after the 2019 circular; confirm any superseding or rail-specific rule.
3. Current 2024 payment-system operator cyber-security directions and amendments, mapped to entity scope.
4. Current DPDP Act/rules commencement and fintech applicability as of the specific question date.
5. Current tax, payment limits, charges, complaint deadlines, credit-reporting timelines, and insurance grievance timelines. These are intentionally not hardcoded as timeless rules.
6. Bank-, PSP-, processor-, network-, and insurer-specific API/file protocols; these are often contractual or proprietary and vary.

SOURCE USE RULE
An official URL in this register is a starting point, not a blanket citation. For a current legal answer, cite the exact instrument and effective date. If retrieval only supplies a general overview, the chatbot should state that the specific rule was not verified.
