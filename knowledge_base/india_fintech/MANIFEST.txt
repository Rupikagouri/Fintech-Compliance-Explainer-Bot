PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This collection explains Indian fintech products, infrastructure, software, operations, controls, and applicable regulation. It excludes generic personal finance, investment tutorials, standalone market education, and unrelated corporate finance. WealthTech content is restricted to product/system workflows and operational controls.

STATUS KEY
COMPLETE = explanatory baseline implemented; not a claim that every edge case or current circular is exhaustively covered.
PARTIAL = core workflow covered; listed volatile rules, institution-specific procedures, or specialist details need further source verification.
SOURCE-VERIFIED = primary source/page checked on 2026-10-09; not a guarantee that no later amendment exists.
ILLUSTRATIVE = architecture, accounting mapping, or example describes a common model and is not a universal implementation.

INGESTION
The primary loader reads UTF-8 .txt files recursively from data/ and knowledge_base/india_fintech/. It strips this front matter at the --- delimiter, preserves metadata, and adds a relative source path. It creates an in-memory FAISS index at app startup; there is no persisted or incremental index to migrate. Restart/rebuild the app after editing documents.

LIMITATIONS
This is a broad explanatory foundation, not a legal opinion, complete rulebook, bank-specific runbook, or source of live transaction status. Check the relevant regulator, rail, contract, and effective date before operational decisions. Refer to SOURCE_REGISTER.txt for verification gaps.
