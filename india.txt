India's nominal Gross Domestic Product (GDP) in 2024 was approximately $3.91 trillion USD, making it one of the largest economies in the world. This represented a significant increase from the previous year and reflected the country's strong economic growth driven by domestic consumption, services, manufacturing, infrastructure investment, and digital transformation.

With a population of about 1.43 billion people, India's GDP per capita was approximately $2,600 USD in 2024. The economy grew at an estimated rate of around 7.1%, making India one of the fastest-growing major economies globally.

In Indian currency terms, India's GDP was approximately ₹324 lakh crore, based on an average exchange rate of around ₹83 per U.S. dollar. The services sector remained the largest contributor to GDP, followed by industry and agriculture.

India's strong economic performance in 2024 was supported by expanding infrastructure projects, increasing foreign investment, a growing digital economy, and rising consumer demand. As a result, India continued to strengthen its position as a major global economic power and remained on track toward becoming a multi-trillion-dollar economy in the coming years.
